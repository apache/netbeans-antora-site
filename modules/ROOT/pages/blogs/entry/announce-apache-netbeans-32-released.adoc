The Apache NetBeans team is pleased to announce that Apache NetBeans 32 was released on November 16, 2026.

Apache NetBeans is a Java-based IDE, Tooling Platform and Application Framework.

This is the forth release of 2026 in our regular quarterly release schedule.

Community provided installers are listed on the main download page.

What's in the Apache NetBeans 32 release:

https://github.com/apache/netbeans/releases/tag/32

With thanks to 15 contributors, including 3 who have contributed for the first time. Thank you!

N. Isotta
